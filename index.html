How to use an ACBuy spreadsheet

01 / DISCOVER

Save the exact item

Find a product in the catalog and record its item ID, seller listing and chosen variation. A spreadsheet row may lead to a catalog page or agent link rather than the original marketplace listing.

02 / COMPARE

Check the details that matter

Review materials, measurements, seller terms and available photos. For Taobao, Weidian or 1688 links, confirm that your selected service can purchase that specific listing.

03 / PLAN

Calculate the full cost

Allow for domestic delivery, service fees, international freight and applicable taxes. If inspection is available, review your own item’s QC photos and return window before arranging shipment.

A useful comparison beats a long link list

What to check before ordering

  • Sizing: compare garment measurements with something you already own. A size label alone is not enough.
  • Variation: confirm color, dimensions and selected option; the lowest advertised price may refer to another variant.
  • Photos: distinguish seller images, another buyer’s inspection photos and photos of your own item.
  • Returns: check eligibility, deadlines and who pays return delivery before purchasing.
  • Delivery: ask about destination coverage, restrictions, tracking and chargeable weight.

ACBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Is this an official ACBuy website?

No. This is an independent product-discovery guide. The featured catalog references come from HipobuyFind. This page does not operate ACBuy accounts, process payments or provide shipping services.

Where do the ACBuy spreadsheet and Buy it now buttons go?

Both buttons lead to https://hipobuyfind.com. Product cards link to the corresponding HipobuyFind item pages so you can review a specific entry. Opening a link does not submit an order.

Are the products imported from a real catalog?

Yes. The four entries above were read from public HipobuyFind product pages on 19 November 2026. Each card includes its source item ID and product-page link. This is a selected static import, not the entire catalog or an automatic synchronization.

Are these products QC checked?

This page has not independently inspected these items. Catalog images are not a substitute for inspection photos of your actual order. If your chosen service offers QC, ask for measurements and close-up photos relevant to your item.

Can every listed product be purchased through ACBuy?

That has not been confirmed. A catalog link does not guarantee that a purchasing service currently accepts the item, seller or marketplace. Verify current service availability, product restrictions and payment terms directly before ordering.

Does the displayed price include shipping and taxes?

No. Displayed amounts are item-price references from the source snapshot. Your total can include domestic shipping, purchasing fees, exchange-rate differences, international freight and destination taxes.

What should I do with a broken or outdated product link?

Record the catalog item ID, revisit the full catalog and compare the seller details. Do not assume that a similar title identifies the same item, seller or variant. Confirm availability before making a payment.
